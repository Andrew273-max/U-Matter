Homepage

U Matter is a platform aimed at organizing and securing volunteering activities for Ukraine.
Our goal is to help volunteers across both Ukraine and Europe to have a structured approach to their work, have the ability of easily contacting potential collaborators, and raise awareness about the ongoing war in Ukraine.

Our impact

Instead of scattered posts and missed opportunities, everything is in one place. It makes it easier to act, even on a busy day. Easier to care, in a practical way. Small actions add up, and slowly, support for Ukraine from across the world stops feeling distant. It becomes something organized, visible, something real.
And that makes all the difference.
